Technical Consulting Services

Senior engineering team solving your equipment challenges

01 Equipment Sizing & Selection

Based on material properties, throughput requirement and layout constraints, we deliver optimized equipment configuration proposals. Avoid over‑specification waste or under‑capacity production interruptions.

02 Failure Diagnosis & Analysis

Treat abnormal vibration, belt deviation, slipping, noise and other typical faults. Remote or on‑site diagnosis, identify root causes quickly, provide repair proposals and required spare‑part lists.

03 Plant Retrofit & Upgrade

Outdated low‑efficiency high‑energy‑consumption equipment? We support automation upgrade, drive‑system modification and dust‑suppression retrofitting. Extend equipment service life and boost production capacity.

04 Reverse‑Engineering & Custom Parts

For imported equipment or components without original drawings, we provide on‑site 3D scanning and CAD modelling to guarantee full interchangeability with original installations.

Over Ten Years Focused on Bulk Material Handling

We are a specialized supplier focusing on bulk‑material‑handling spare parts and engineering services. We serve global customers across mining, port terminals, thermal power plants, cement plants and steel mills.

Our scope includes belt conveyor, bucket‑wheel stacker‑reclaimer and heavy‑duty apron feeder spare‑parts supply and complete‑unit manufacturing. We also deliver equipment sizing, fault diagnosis and retrofitting consulting. Fully aware of production losses caused by equipment downtime, we follow the principle: Quality‑First, Fast Response.

All spare‑parts go through strict quality inspection. Critical components are supplied with material certificates and NDT test reports. Core wear‑parts are kept in stock to support urgent shipment for emergency breakdown cases.
